Our Story · 物語

Ichigoichie.

一期一会 — “one time, one meeting.” Every bowl is a moment that will never happen quite the same way again.

茶の湯

For centuries, chanoyu — the way of tea — has turned the simplest act into something sacred.

Warm the bowl. Sift the powder. Listen to the whisk. Japanese tea culture asks you to slow down and pay attention: to the colour, the foam, the first sip. Ocha no Pauda — literally “tea powder” — brings that ritual to your counter without the ceremony being a barrier. Imperial and ceremonial matcha, an honest bamboo whisk, and the oat milk that makes it sing.
